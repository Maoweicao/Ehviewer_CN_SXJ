## What is EhViewer CN SXJ?

**EhViewer CN SXJ** is a feature-rich E-Hentai browser for Android. It lets you browse, search, download, and manage galleries from E-Hentai and ExHentai, all through a polished Chinese-localized interface with English support.

### Key Features at a Glance

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Gallery Browsing | Browse homepage, subscriptions, hot galleries, and top lists |
| Powerful Search | Keyword, tag, uploader, and image-based search with advanced filters |
| Download Manager | Queue, pause, resume, and organize downloads with labels |
| Favorites | 10 cloud folders + 1 local folder for organizing your collection |
| History | Automatic browsing history with swipe-to-delete |
| Gallery Reader | Three reading directions, zoom modes, auto-page-turn, and PiP |
| Themes | Light, dark, and AMOLED black themes |
| AI Translation | Experimental in-image text translation |

## Installation

1. Download the latest APK from the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/xiaojieonly/Ehviewer_CN_SXJ/releases) page.
2. On your Android device, enable **Install from unknown sources** if prompted.
3. Open the APK file and follow the on-screen instructions.

> **Note:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher is recommended.

## First Launch

When you open the app for the first time, you'll go through a short setup flow:

1. **Security Lock** (optional) — Set up a fingerprint or PIN lock.
2. **Warning Page** — Acknowledge the content disclaimer.
3. **Analytics Consent** — Choose whether to share anonymous usage data.
4. **Sign In** — Log in with your E-Hentai account. You can skip this and sign in later from Settings.
5. **Site Selection** — Choose between E-Hentai and ExHentai (requires separate account).

<!-- screenshot: docs/help/en/index-setup-flow.png -->

After setup, you'll land on the **Homepage** showing the latest galleries.

## Basic Usage

### Browsing Galleries

- Scroll through the gallery list on any page.
- Tap a gallery card to open its **detail page** with title, tags, rating, and previews.
- Tap **Read** to open the gallery in the built-in reader.

### Searching

- Tap the **search bar** at the top of the gallery list.
- Type keywords, tags (e.g. `artist:name`), or paste a URL.
- Use the **filter icon** to narrow results by category, rating, or advanced options.

### Downloading

- On a gallery's detail page, tap the **Download** button.
- Long-press the Download button to choose a download label/category.
- View and manage all downloads from the **Downloads** section in the navigation drawer.

### Favoriting

- On a gallery's detail page, tap the **heart icon** or use the FAB menu.
- Choose which favorites folder to save to (10 cloud + 1 local).
- Access your favorites from the **Favorites** section in the navigation drawer.

## Tips

- **Clipboard monitoring**: Copy an E-Hentai URL and the app will detect it automatically, offering to open the gallery.
- **Long-press FAB**: On list screens, long-press the floating action button to enter multi-select mode for batch operations.
- **Theme toggle**: Tap the theme button at the bottom of the navigation drawer to cycle through Light → Dark → Black themes.
- **Avatar & wallpaper**: Tap the profile image in the navigation drawer header to customize your avatar; tap the background to set a custom wallpaper (supports GIF).
